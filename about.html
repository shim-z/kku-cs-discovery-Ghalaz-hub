Department overview

Computer science at King Khalid University

The Department of Computer Science was established in the academic year 2000/2001 under the College of Computer Science at King Khalid University.

It offers a four-year Bachelor in Computer Science program structured around 138 credits, alongside postgraduate programs. The Bachelor program includes tracks in Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering and Cybersecurity.
