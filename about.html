Department overview

Computer science at King Khalid University

The Department of Computer Science was established in the academic year 2000/2001 under the College of Computer Science at King Khalid University.

It offers a four-year Bachelor in Computer Science program structured around 138 credits, alongside postgraduate programs. The department’s study pathways include Intelligent Systems, Software Engineering and Network Security.
